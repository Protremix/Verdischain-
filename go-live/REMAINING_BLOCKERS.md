## 1. Executive Summary & Launch Gate Status

Verdis Chain has reached full internal technical readiness on Testnet. The Substrate Layer-1 core architecture is fully implemented across 16 custom pallets, validated by 503 unit and integration tests, and optimized with complete weight files for benchmarking. All 8 internal executive decisions were formally approved by Rojs on August 14, 2026. Complete internal preparation packages—including legal briefing packages, security RFPs, key ceremony specifications, independence test frameworks, claims registers, incident response playbooks, bug bounty programs, and status page infrastructure—have been finalized.

However, **Mainnet Launch and Public Token Sale REMAIN STRICTLY BLOCKED**. There are **7 OPEN external blockers** that require engagement, contractual execution, legal qualification, or physical witness attestations from external third parties.

### Core Governance Principles & Rules:
1. **No False Resolutions:** No blocker shall be marked as resolved until all required external evidence is formally delivered, reviewed, and archived.
2. **Strict Launch Gate Rule:** **DO NOT RECOMMEND LAUNCH IF ANY P0 BLOCKER REMAINS OPEN.** Mainnet genesis instantiation and public token sale deployment are strictly frozen.
3. **External Party Dependency:** All 7 remaining blockers require external legal counsel, security audit firms, corporate registries, KYC vendors, or physical key ceremony witnesses.
4. **Rojs Executive Signature Mandate:** Unanimous sign-off and explicit written authorization from Rojs is required for each blocker resolution and final go-live approval.
