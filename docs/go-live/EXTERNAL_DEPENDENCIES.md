# External Dependencies

## Purpose

This document identifies launch dependencies that cannot be closed by repository changes alone.

A documentation entry does not constitute legal, regulatory, compliance, security or executive approval.

## P0 — UAE / VARA Legal

**Owner:** Executive / legal counsel

- [ ] Engage qualified UAE counsel with relevant VARA expertise.
- [ ] Confirm the legal entity structure.
- [ ] Determine whether the proposed activities require VARA authorization, registration, licensing or another regulatory treatment.
- [ ] Review the proposed VRDX offering and distribution model.
- [ ] Document applicable restrictions and permitted activities.
- [ ] Obtain written legal advice.
- [ ] Archive the final legal opinion.

**Status:** BLOCKED until qualified counsel provides the required analysis.

## P0 — EU / MiCA

**Owner:** Executive / EU legal counsel

- [ ] Engage qualified EU counsel.
- [ ] Provide counsel with the final token design and intended offering model.
- [ ] Obtain a written VRDX classification analysis.
- [ ] Determine whether MiCA applies to the proposed activity.
- [ ] Determine the applicable issuer/offeror obligations.
- [ ] Determine applicable white-paper and notification requirements.
- [ ] Determine marketing and distribution restrictions.
- [ ] Document any country-specific restrictions.
- [ ] Archive the legal opinion.

**Status:** BLOCKED until qualified counsel provides the required analysis.

## P0 — Global Jurisdiction Policy

**Owner:** Executive / legal counsel

- [ ] Build a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction matrix.
- [ ] Identify prohibited jurisdictions.
- [ ] Identify restricted jurisdictions.
- [ ] Identify jurisdictions requiring additional controls.
- [ ] Define geo-blocking requirements where applicable.
- [ ] Define KYC/KYB requirements by jurisdiction.
- [ ] Define sanctions screening requirements.
- [ ] Define marketing restrictions.
- [ ] Obtain legal review of the final policy.
- [ ] Approve and version the policy.

**Status:** BLOCKED until legal review is complete.
