1. Abstract

Verdis is the world's first fully green blockchain ecosystem — a Layer-1 chain designed from the ground up to be carbon-negative. Unlike other blockchains that merely reduce energy consumption, Verdis makes environmental regeneration a core protocol feature. The chain combines Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS) consensus with an integrated Automated Market Maker (AMM) decentralized exchange, on-chain carbon credit minting, reforestation tracking, and green validator scoring.

The native token, VRS (Verdis), has a maximum supply of 100 billion tokens with 50 billion currently in circulation. Verdis operates on Chain ID 909 with Ethereum-compatible JSON-RPC, enabling direct integration with MetaMask and Trust Wallet. The ecosystem features a native wallet that works without browser extensions, a full block explorer, governance, NFT support, and a stack-based smart contract virtual machine.

3. Problem Statement

The blockchain industry faces three critical challenges: environmental impact, accessibility, and fragmented DeFi.

3.1 Environmental Impact

Traditional Proof-of-Work blockchains consume enormous amounts of energy. Bitcoin alone uses more electricity than many countries. While Proof-of-Stake has reduced energy consumption, no major chain has made environmental regeneration a core protocol feature. Carbon offsetting remains an afterthought — a marketing claim rather than a verifiable, on-chain mechanism.

3.2 Accessibility

Most blockchains require users to install browser extensions (MetaMask), manage seed phrases across multiple apps, and navigate complex bridging interfaces. This creates friction that prevents mainstream adoption of both blockchain technology and green initiatives.

3.3 Fragmented DeFi

DEXes exist as separate protocols on most chains, requiring additional smart contracts, gas fees, and trust assumptions. There is no native connection between DeFi and environmental impact.

4. The Verdis Solution

Verdis addresses these challenges through five core innovations that together make it the first fully green blockchain ecosystem:

4.1 Carbon-Negative by Design

Verdis tracks carbon credits on-chain, logs reforestation projects, and scores validators based on their use of renewable energy. The chain provides verifiable, immutable proof of environmental impact — not marketing claims.

4.2 Green Validator Scoring

Validators on the Verdis network are scored based on their use of renewable energy. This is the first blockchain where a validator's environmental footprint directly affects their protocol standing.

4.3 Eco-Token Economy

Verdis features native eco-tokens (CARBON, ECO, GREEN, TREE, REDD) that represent real environmental assets. These tokens trade on the built-in DEX, creating a market for environmental impact.

4.4 Native Wallet Experience

The Verdis dashboard includes a built-in native wallet. Users can create, import, and manage wallets directly in the browser without installing any extension. MetaMask and Trust Wallet remain supported as optional integrations.

4.5 Protocol-Level DEX

The AMM-based DEX is built into the chain itself, not deployed as a separate smart contract. This means lower fees, no contract risk, and immediate liquidity for all native tokens including eco-tokens.

5.3 State Persistence

All chain state — blocks, balances, DEX pools, validators, contracts, eco data (carbon credits, reforestation projects, green scores) — is persisted to disk. The chain survives full node restarts and automatically resumes block production.

6. DPoS Consensus — Green by Design

Verdis uses Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS), the most energy-efficient consensus mechanism suitable for a fully green blockchain. Unlike Proof-of-Work which requires massive energy expenditure, DPoS uses negligible electricity while maintaining full decentralization and security.

6.1 Validator System

  • 27 validators registered on the network
  • 5 active validators produce blocks in round-robin rotation
  • Block producers change every block, rotating through the active set
  • 16 VRS block reward distributed per block
  • Block production interval: 5 seconds

6.2 Green Validator Scoring

This is a Verdis-first feature. Validators receive a green score based on:

  • Energy source — solar, wind, hydro, geothermal verified
  • Carbon footprint of validator operations
  • Verified renewable energy certificates

Higher green scores increase validator visibility and community trust. This is the first blockchain where environmental footprint directly affects protocol participation.

6.3 Delegation & Rewards

Token holders can delegate VRS to any registered validator. Delegators earn a portion of the block rewards proportional to their stake.

6.4 Slashing

Validators can be slashed for double-signing, downtime, and invalid block production.

10.1 Carbon Credit System

Users can mint carbon credits on-chain by providing verified CO2 offset data. Each credit is an immutable on-chain record containing amount of CO2 offset, project description, minter address, timestamp, and retirement status. Carbon credits can be traded on the DEX as CARBON tokens, creating the first on-chain market for verified carbon offsets.

10.2 Reforestation Tracking

Reforestation projects are logged on-chain with immutable proof: number of trees planted, geographic location, project initiator, date, and verification metadata. 15,000 trees are already logged. Each tree is permanently recorded on the Verdis blockchain — it cannot be deleted, altered, or falsified.

10.3 Green Validator Scoring

Validators receive a green score based on their use of renewable energy. This is the first blockchain protocol where environmental footprint affects consensus participation. Higher green score = greater community trust and visibility.

10.4 Carbon Offset Fund

5 billion VRS (5% of max supply) is allocated to the Carbon Offset Fund — the first dedicated on-chain fund for environmental regeneration in blockchain history. This fund finances reforestation projects worldwide, carbon capture initiatives, renewable energy infrastructure for validators, and verified carbon credit purchases.
