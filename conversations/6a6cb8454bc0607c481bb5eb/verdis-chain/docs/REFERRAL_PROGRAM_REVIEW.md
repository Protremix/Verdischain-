## LEGAL AND REGULATORY RISK ASSESSMENT

### Multi-Level Marketing (MLM) Classification Risk

The 3-tier referral structure (10%/5%/2.5%) with earnings from indirect referrals may be classified as a multi-level marketing scheme in certain jurisdictions.

**Risks:**
- **United States:** FTC regulates MLM structures; may require registration and disclosures
- **European Union:** MiCA may classify referral rewards as a marketing practice subject to restrictions
- **United Kingdom:** MLM regulations may apply
- **Other jurisdictions:** Varies by country

### Securities Law Risk

If referral rewards are based on the investment returns of referred users, the referral program may be considered a form of investment solicitation, potentially triggering securities registration requirements.

### Tax Implications

Referral commissions may be taxable as:
- Ordinary income (commissions)
- Capital gains (token appreciation)
- Business income (if operating as a referral business)

Each recipient may have tax obligations in their jurisdiction.
