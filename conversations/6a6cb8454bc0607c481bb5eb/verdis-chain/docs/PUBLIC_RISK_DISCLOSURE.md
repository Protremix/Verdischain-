## IMPORTANT RISK WARNING

Participation in the Verdis Chain ecosystem, including purchasing VRDX tokens, using the DEX, staking, or interacting with smart contracts, carries significant risks. You should carefully consider these risks before participating.

---

## 1. NETWORK STATUS RISK

**Current network status: DEVELOPMENT CHAIN / TESTNET**

- The Verdis Chain is currently running as a development chain.
- VRDX devnet tokens have **NO MONETARY VALUE**.
- Devnet state may be **RESET AT ANY TIME** without notice.
- **Mainnet is NOT live.**
- Mainnet launch is PLANNED but has no confirmed date.

## 2. TOKEN PRICE RISK

- VRDX token price is subject to market volatility.
- The TGE price of $0.005 is a **TARGET**, not a guaranteed price.
- Presale discounts do NOT guarantee profit.
- You may lose some or all of your investment.

## 3. SMART CONTRACT RISK

- Smart contracts on Verdis Chain have NOT been independently audited.
- Smart contracts may contain bugs, vulnerabilities, or exploits.
- You may lose tokens due to contract failures.

## 4. VALIDATOR AND CONSENSUS RISK

- The network currently has 6 validators (devnet), which is highly centralized.
- The target of 21 validators for mainnet has not been achieved.
- Slashing may result in loss of staked tokens.

## 5. FUNDRAISING RISK

- No funds have been raised as of the date of this document.
- "Total Raised: $18M" on the sale page is a **TARGET**, not actual funds received.
- Tokens may not be listed on any exchange.
- There is no guarantee of liquidity after TGE.

## 6. REGULATORY RISK

- The legal entity responsible for Verdis Chain is not publicly documented.
- No regulatory compliance review (including MiCA) has been completed.
- VRDX tokens may be classified as securities in some jurisdictions.

## 7. REFERRAL PROGRAM RISK

- The 3-tier referral program (10%/5%/2.5%) has NOT been reviewed by legal counsel.
- The program may be classified as a multi-level marketing scheme in some jurisdictions.
- Referral rewards are NOT investment returns.

## 8. TREASURY RISK

- Treasury is controlled by a PalletId (code-based account), not a real multisig.
- Treasury management rules are not publicly documented.

## 9. TECHNOLOGY RISK

- The Verdis Chain codebase has 689 tests but has NOT been independently audited.
- Performance has NOT been benchmarked.
- The network has 0 peers, indicating it is not decentralized.

## 10. ENVIRONMENTAL CLAIM RISK

- "Carbon Negative" and "Green Blockchain" claims are DESIGN OBJECTIVES, not certified facts.
- Environmental claims have NOT been independently verified.

## 11. AI AND SECURITY CLAIM RISK

- No AI system currently audits, verifies, or secures smart contracts on Verdis Chain.
- "AI audit" claims are PLANNED, not implemented.

## 12. VESTING AND UNLOCK RISK

- Vesting schedules may result in significant token unlocks that could depress prices.
- The unlock schedule may create market pressure at unlock events.

## 13. AUDIT LIMITATIONS

- No independent security audit has been performed.
- Internal reviews cover code quality but do not constitute a security audit.

## 14. CENTRALIZATION RISK

- The network currently operates with 6 validators, all using well-known test keys.
- The project is developed by a small team.
- Key decisions are made by the founder.
- Treasury is controlled by a PalletId, not a distributed multisig.
