Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about the Verdis Token Sale? We have answers.

What is Verdis and why is it carbon-negative?
Verdis is a high-performance Layer-1 blockchain engineered for net-zero sustainability. Using Green DPoS consensus and satellite-tracked reforestation, Verdis actively plants real-world trees for every block produced, offsetting 110% of its electricity consumption.
When will the Token Sale end and when is TGE?
Public Sale Stage 2 will conclude when the stage allocation is filled or when the countdown timer expires. The Token Generation Event (TGE) and initial DEX listing on Uniswap and VerdisSwap are scheduled immediately following the completion of Stage 3.
What are the minimum and maximum purchase amounts?
The minimum contribution per transaction is $50 USD equivalent, and the maximum purchase per individual wallet is $100,000 USD equivalent during Stage 2 to prevent token concentration.
Which crypto currencies and wallets are supported?
You can purchase $VRS using Ethereum (ETH), BNB Chain (BNB), Tether (USDT), or USD Coin (USDC). Supported Web3 wallets include MetaMask, WalletConnect, Trust Wallet, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, and SafePal.
How does the vesting schedule work for IDO buyers?
Public Sale investors unlock 20% of their $VRS tokens immediately at TGE. The remaining 80% vests linearly over 6 months with monthly claimable distributions directly through our vesting portal.
Is the Verdis token contract audited?
Yes, our smart contracts have been thoroughly audited by CertiK and Hacken. Zero critical or high-risk vulnerabilities were identified. Full audit reports are publicly downloadable on our GitHub and whitepaper page.
