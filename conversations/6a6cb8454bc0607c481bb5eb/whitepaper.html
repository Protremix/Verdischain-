1. Abstract

Verdis is a Layer-1 blockchain designed from the ground up to be carbon-negative. It combines Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS) consensus with an integrated Automated Market Maker (AMM) decentralized exchange, on-chain carbon credit minting, reforestation tracking, and green validator scoring. The chain operates on Chain ID 909 with Ethereum-compatible JSON-RPC, enabling direct integration with MetaMask and Trust Wallet.

The native token, VRS (Verdis), has a maximum supply of 100 billion tokens with 50 billion currently in circulation. Verdis features a native wallet system that works without browser extensions, a full block explorer, governance, NFT support, and a stack-based smart contract virtual machine.

2. Problem Statement

The blockchain industry faces two critical challenges: environmental impact and accessibility.

2.1 Environmental Impact

Traditional Proof-of-Work blockchains consume enormous amounts of energy. Bitcoin alone uses more electricity than many countries. While Proof-of-Stake has reduced energy consumption, no major chain has made environmental regeneration a core protocol feature. Carbon offsetting remains an afterthought — a marketing tool rather than a fundamental mechanism.

2.2 Accessibility

Most blockchains require users to install browser extensions (MetaMask), manage seed phrases across multiple apps, and navigate complex bridging interfaces. This creates friction that prevents mainstream adoption.

2.3 Fragmented DeFi

DEXes exist as separate protocols on most chains, requiring additional smart contracts, gas fees, and trust assumptions. Verdis integrates the DEX directly into the chain protocol layer.

3. The Verdis Solution

Verdis addresses these challenges through four core innovations:

3.1 Carbon-Negative by Design

Verdis tracks carbon credits on-chain, logs reforestation projects, and scores validators based on their use of renewable energy. The chain doesn't just claim to be green — it provides verifiable, immutable proof of environmental impact.

3.2 Native Wallet Experience

The Verdis dashboard includes a built-in native wallet. Users can create, import, and manage wallets directly in the browser without installing any extension. MetaMask and Trust Wallet remain supported as optional integrations via EIP-1193 and JSON-RPC.

3.3 Protocol-Level DEX

The AMM-based DEX is built into the chain itself, not deployed as a separate smart contract. This means lower fees, no contract risk, and immediate liquidity for all native tokens.

3.4 Integrated Token Sale (IDO)

Verdis includes a native token sale mechanism, allowing users to purchase VRS directly with ETH, BNB, USDT, or USDC without third-party platforms.

4.3 State Persistence

All chain state — blocks, balances, DEX pools, validators, contracts, eco data — is persisted to disk. The chain survives full node restarts and automatically resumes block production.

5. DPoS Consensus

Verdis uses Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS), a consensus mechanism where token holders delegate their stake to validators who produce blocks on their behalf.

5.1 Validator System

  • 27 validators registered on the network
  • 5 active validators produce blocks in round-robin rotation
  • Block producers change every block, rotating through the active set
  • 16 VRS block reward distributed per block
  • Block production interval: 5 seconds

5.2 Delegation & Rewards

Token holders can delegate VRS to any registered validator. Delegators earn a portion of the block rewards proportional to their stake. The system supports delegation, undelegation, automatic reward distribution, and validator commission rates.

5.3 Slashing

Validators can be slashed for malicious behavior, including double-signing, downtime, and invalid block production.

8.2 AMM Formula

The DEX uses the constant product formula: x * y = k

Δy = y * Δx / (x + Δx) * (1 - fee)

With a 0.3% fee applied to input amount, distributed to liquidity providers.

8.3 Liquidity Provision

Users can provide liquidity to any pool by depositing both tokens in the correct ratio. In return, they receive LP tokens representing their share of the pool.

9.1 Carbon Credit System

Users can mint carbon credits on-chain by providing verified CO₂ offset data. Each credit includes amount of CO₂ offset, project description, minter address, timestamp, and retirement status.

9.2 Reforestation Tracking

Reforestation projects can be logged on-chain with immutable proof: number of trees planted, geographic location, project initiator, date, and verification metadata.

9.3 Green Validator Scoring

Validators receive a green score based on their use of renewable energy. Higher green scores increase validator visibility and community trust. The system tracks energy source, carbon footprint, and verified renewable energy certificates.
