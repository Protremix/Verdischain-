<div class='['section']'> Category Breakdown Detailed Category Analysis Each allocation serves a specific purpose in the Verdis Chain ecosystem. 1. Community Rewards 25% (25B VRDX) Purpose: Rewards for DPoS validators, nominators, and community contributors securing the network via BABE/GRANDPA consensus. Distribution: Emitted continuously via Substrate runtime block reward pallets. Staking rewards distributed per epoch. Economic Reasoning: Sustains network cryptoeconomic security over decades. Incentivizes long-term participation and honest validator behavior. Vesting Per-block epoch emissions Governance Rewards active nominators 2. Eco Fund 18% (18B VRDX) Purpose: Funds carbon credit minting, reforestation logging, green validator scoring, and environmental verification partnerships. Distribution: Released via on-chain governance proposals for verified eco-projects and carbon offset initiatives. Economic Reasoning: Ensures Verdis Chain remains carbon-negative. Ties token utility to real-world environmental impact. Vesting Governance-controlled Governance DAO eco-proposals 3. Team & Advisors 15% (15B VRDX) Purpose: Aligns founding engineers, Substrate core developers, advisors, and contributors with long-term network success. Distribution: 12-month cliff, then linear monthly vesting over 24 months. Economic Reasoning: Extended lockup prevents founder dump risk and ensures long-term commitment to the Verdis Chain architecture. Vesting 12mo cliff + 24mo linear Governance Standard voting power 4. DEX Liquidity 15% (15B VRDX) Purpose: Provides seed liquidity for native AMM DEX pools (VRDX/ECO, VRDX/CARBON, VRDX/TREE, and additional pairs). Distribution: Unlocked at mainnet launch to guarantee AMM pool depth and minimize slippage. Economic Reasoning: Prevents price slippage, supports seamless user swaps, and ensures healthy market formation from day one. Vesting Unlocked at DEX launch Governance Non-voting liquidity 5. Treasury Reserve 15% (15B VRDX) Purpose: Funds protocol development, security audits, ecosystem grants, and emergency response. Managed transparently via on-chain DAO. Distribution: Governance-controlled release schedule. All expenditures require on-chain referendum approval. Economic Reasoning: Provides long-term capital runway with full transparency. Community controls allocation through governance. Vesting DAO referendum releases Governance Community-controlled 6. Investors (IDO) 12% (12B VRDX) Purpose: Capital backing for infrastructure deployment, security audits, and institutional ecosystem partnerships. Distribution: 4 IDO phases — Phase 1 (Seed, 3B at $0.0005), Phase 2 (Private, 3B at $0.001), Phase 3 (Presale, 4B at $0.002), Phase 4 (Public, 2B at $0.0025). Economic Reasoning: Secures foundational liquidity and strategic validation. Discounted pricing rewards early supporters. Hard cap $17.5M. Vesting Phase-dependent (see below) Governance Standard voting rights
