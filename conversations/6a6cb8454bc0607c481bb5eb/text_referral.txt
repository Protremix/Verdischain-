How It Works

Start earning VRDX in 3 simple steps.

1
Share Your Link

Copy your unique referral link and share it on social media, messaging apps, or your website. Anyone who clicks becomes your referral.

2
Friends Buy VRDX

When your friends purchase VRDX tokens through your link, the transaction is automatically tracked and attributed to your account.

3
You Earn Commission

Receive 10% commission instantly. When your referrals share their own links, you earn 5% and 2.5% from Tier 2 and Tier 3 purchases.

FAQ
How are commissions paid?
▾
Commissions are paid in VRDX tokens directly to your wallet at the Token Generation Event (TGE). You'll receive a single payment covering all tiers with your regular token allocation.
Is there a limit on referrals?
▾
No. You can refer as many people as you want. The more referrals, the more you earn. Start sharing your link early to maximize your earnings when the IDO goes live.
Do referrals expire?
▾
Referrals are valid for 90 days after someone clicks your link. If they purchase within that window, you earn commission. After 90 days, the cookie expires.
Can I track my referrals?
▾
Yes. Your dashboard will show total referrals, conversion rate, earnings breakdown by tier, and weekly activity charts. All updated in real-time once the IDO begins.
