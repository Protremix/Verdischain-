Substrate Pallet Protocol

How Verdis AMM Works

Built as a core Substrate runtime pallet in Rust (`pallet-amm`), eliminating contract deployment costs and securing liquidity at the protocol consensus layer.

k

Constant Product Invariant

The DEX enforces the constant product formula x * y = k. When token A is swapped for token B, the product of liquidity reserves remains constant, automatically determining output prices based on supply and demand.

⚡

Protocol-Level Execution

Unlike EVM smart contracts that require virtual machine bytecode interpretation and gas meter checks, Verdis AMM executes natively inside Substrate runtime WebAssembly at 1,000+ TPS.

  • Zero EVM gas surcharges — only base runtime weight fee
  • Immune to EVM reentrancy or flash-loan exploit vectors
  • Direct integration with Substrate Balance and Carbon Pallets
  • Atomic multi-swap routing across all native trading pairs
💎

LP Tokens & Fee Rewards

When you supply liquidity, the protocol mints fungible LP tokens representing your proportional share of the pool. LP tokens accumulate 0.25% trading fees automatically as pool reserves grow.

Fee Breakdown: 0.25% auto-compounded to LPs + 0.05% routed to Verdis Carbon Credit Treasury for net-zero sustainability.
🛡️

Impermanent Loss Protection

Verdis Chain incorporates dynamic fee scaling and carbon credit yield subsidies to mitigate impermanent loss for long-term liquidity providers during high price volatility events.

Eco Incentive: LPs providing liquidity for green tokens (`VRD/cVRD`) earn extra carbon credit staking rewards up to 18.5% APR.
