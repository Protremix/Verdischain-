Frequently Asked Questions




What is VRDX? 
▾


VERDIS (VRDX) is the native utility token of Verdis Chain, an eco-friendly Layer-1 blockchain built with Substrate. It's used for transaction fees, staking, governance, DEX trading, and carbon credit transactions. Max supply is 100 billion tokens. This is a utility token, not an investment vehicle.






When will tokens be distributed? 
▾


Tokens are distributed at the Token Generation Event (TGE). Seed investors have a 12-month cliff, then monthly vesting over 24 months. TGE circulating supply: 8B (8% of max supply). FDV at TGE: $500M.






What is the minimum investment? 
▾


The minimum investment for Round 1 (Seed) varies by round: Seed requires strategic commitment, Community has lower minimums, and Public Presale has a $100 minimum with $25,000 maximum per wallet. KYC is required for all rounds.






How do I receive my tokens? 
▾


Provide your Verdis Chain wallet address when purchasing. Tokens will be sent to this address at TGE. You can create a wallet at our 
Verdis Wallet
 page.






Is there a bonus for early investors? 
▾


No bonus tokens are offered. Instead, early investors receive a discount to TGE price: Seed at 70% discount ($0.0015), Community at 40% discount ($0.003), and Presale at 20% discount ($0.004). The TGE/IDO price is $0.005. This structure is more transparent and sustainable than bonus schemes.






Is there a lock-up period? 
▾


Yes. Vesting varies by phase. Seed and Private Sale tokens have a 12-month cliff with 0% at TGE, then monthly unlocks over 24 months. Presale tokens have 25% unlocked at TGE, a 3-month cliff, then 6.25% per month for 12 months. Public Sale tokens have 40% at TGE, then 10% per month for 6 months. This prevents immediate dumping and protects token price stability.
