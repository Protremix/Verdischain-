1. Mission

VERDIS is the native token of Verdis Blockchain and the wider Evolvix ecosystem. It is designed to support:

Network Security

PoS staking, validator bonding, slashing economics

Transactions

Transaction fees, smart contract execution gas

Ecosystem Services

AI services, AI agents, developer tools, marketplace

The objective is long-term network utility, security, decentralization, and sustainability — NOT speculative price appreciation.

20. Burn Mechanism

Decision: NOT introducing token burning at launch.

Burning should only be introduced if the economic model demonstrates genuine purpose. Current reasoning:

  • Transaction volume is too low to generate meaningful burn
  • Fee revenue goes to treasury and staking rewards
  • Burning reduces staking pool sustainability
  • No demonstrated purpose at current network stage
Burn mechanism will be evaluated after mainnet reaches 10,000+ active users and $100K+ monthly fee revenue. Any burn would require governance approval and economic impact analysis.
