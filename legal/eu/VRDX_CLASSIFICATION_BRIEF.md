## 2. Comprehensive Token Rights Profile

To assist counsel in evaluating whether VRDX constitutes a financial instrument under MiFID II or a specific category under MiCA, the token's exact rights profile is detailed below:

### A. Financial & Economic Rights
- **Dividends or Profit Sharing:** **NONE.** VRDX confers no rights to company revenues, profits, dividends, or liquidation proceeds of Protremix S.L. or any foundation entity.
- **Equity Rights:** **NONE.** VRDX carries no equity ownership, shares, debentures, or debt claim against any entity.
- **Redemption Rights against Issuer:** **NONE.** Neither Protremix S.L. nor the planned Foundation promises or guarantees token buybacks or fiat redemption rights.

### B. Governance & Voting Rights
- **Protocol Governance:** VRDX holders can lock tokens on-chain (`pallet-governance`) to submit referenda, vote on parameter adjustments (e.g., gas fee rates, eco-scoring weights), and approve Wasm runtime code upgrades.
- **Corporate Governance:** **NONE.** VRDX confers no voting rights in Protremix S.L. shareholders' meetings or corporate board decisions.

### C. Utility & Operational Rights
- **Network Gas Fees:** Mandatory payment asset for transaction processing fees across the Layer-1 protocol.
- **Consensus Staking:** Required collateral asset for validator node operation and nominator delegation in `pallet-staking`.
- **DEX Liquidity Base:** Primary base trading asset and liquidity pair currency in the native AMM (`pallet-dex`).
- **TX Relay Fees:** Utility fee payment asset for non-custodial transaction relay signing.
