## 7. Critical Path Analysis & Risk Mitigation

### 7.1 Banking Latency Risk
- **Risk:** Corporate bank account opening in digital asset jurisdictions frequently experiences delays due to compliance backlogs (4 to 8 weeks).
- **Mitigation:** Prepare complete compliance dossier (UBO verification, source of wealth proof, business plan) during Phase 1. Concurrently apply to primary tier-1 crypto banks (e.g., Sygnum, SEBA, AMINA) and tier-2 payment institutions (EMIs).

### 7.2 Regulatory Standstill Requirements
- **Risk:** Frameworks such as EU MiCA enforce a mandatory 28-day standstill window following whitepaper filing before public offering activities can commence.
- **Mitigation:** Finalize whitepaper legal review (TKN-01) early in Phase 3 to allow submission to regulatory bodies immediately upon entity registration.

### 7.3 Intercompany Permanent Establishment Exposure
- **Risk:** Spanish tax authorities may argue that the Offering Entity is effectively managed from Spain if Protremix directors make strategic decisions.
- **Mitigation:** Ensure local resident directors in the chosen jurisdiction hold actual decision-making authority, execute board resolutions locally, and maintain independent corporate record books.

### 7.4 Technical Portal Integration Delays
- **Risk:** Frontend sale portal integration with identity verification APIs or Substrate wallet web3 RPC providers may encounter security bugs during UAT.
- **Mitigation:** Initiate KYC API sandbox testing during Phase 3 alongside compliance documentation reviews.
