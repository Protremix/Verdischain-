Cookie Policy

Contents

1 Introduction

1.1. This Cookie Policy explains how Verdis Chain ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Protremix, uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at verdischain.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1.2. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

2 Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on the Site:

Cookie TypePurposeDuration
EssentialRequired for the Site to function (navigation, security, session management)Session
FunctionalRemember your preferences (theme, language, wallet connection)30 days
AnalyticsTrack usage patterns to improve Site performance and user experience24 hours
PerformanceMonitor RPC endpoint latency and blockchain node healthSession

2.1. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or social media tracking pixels on the Site.

3 Essential Cookies

3.1. Essential cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable core functionality such as network navigation, access to secure areas, and the blockchain RPC connection. The Site cannot function properly without these cookies.

3.2. Essential cookies we use include:

  • session_id — Maintains your session when interacting with the Web Wallet or DEX
  • csrf_token — Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks
  • rpc_endpoint — Remembers your preferred RPC node endpoint

4 Functional Cookies

4.1. Functional cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as your preferred theme, wallet address, or language) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

4.2. These cookies are optional and can be disabled without affecting the core functionality of the Site.

5 Analytics Cookies

5.1. We use self-hosted analytics to understand how visitors interact with the Site. This helps us improve the user experience and optimize performance. All analytics data is anonymized and aggregated.

5.2. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party analytics service that shares data with external parties.

6 Managing Cookies

6.1. You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View all cookies currently stored on your device
  • Block all cookies or specific cookies
  • Clear all cookies when you close your browser
  • Block third-party cookies

6.2. Note that blocking essential cookies may prevent the Site from functioning properly. The Web Wallet, DEX, and Verdiscan explorer require essential cookies to operate.

7 Blockchain-Specific Data

7.1. The Verdis Chain Site uses browser storage specific to blockchain interaction:

  • wallet_address — Stores your connected wallet address (public key only, no private keys are ever stored)
  • network_type — Remembers whether you are connected to testnet or mainnet
  • faucet_cooldown — Tracks faucet request cooldown periods (testnet only)

7.2. We never store private keys, seed phrases, or any sensitive cryptographic material in cookies or browser storage. Wallet connections are made entirely client-side.

8 Third-Party Services

8.1. The Site uses Google Fonts for typography. Google may set cookies when loading fonts. We use the preconnect method to minimize data sent to third parties.

8.2. The Site does not embed third-party widgets, social media buttons, or advertising networks that would set additional cookies.

9 Changes to This Policy

9.1. We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9.2. If we make material changes, we will provide a prominent notice on the Site prior to the change taking effect.
