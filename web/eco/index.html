On-Chain Carbon Credit System

Verdis Chain implements native cVRDX carbon credit tokens. Every cVRDX represents exactly 1 metric ton of verified CO2 greenhouse gas reduction or removal.

🌱

1. Proof-of-Offset Minting

Authorized satellite or IoT sensors verify real-world carbon offset projects (reforestation, direct air capture, renewable energy) and mint immutable cVRDX credit tokens on-chain.

Pallet-Eco :: mint_credit()
🔄

2. Native AMM Trading

Trade cVRDX carbon credits liquidity seamlessly on the Verdis DEX against VRDX, USDT, or ETH with zero slippage or intermediary commissions.

Pallet-DEX :: cVRDX/VRDX
🔥

3. Permanent Retirement

Retire cVRDX tokens by burning them on-chain. Upon burn, a cryptographic NFT certificate is generated verifying your organisation's net-zero contribution.

Pallet-Eco :: retire_credit()
