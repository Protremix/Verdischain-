Frequently Asked Questions

What is VRDX? ▾
VRDX is the native utility token of Verdis Chain, an eco-friendly Layer-1 blockchain built with Substrate. It's used for transaction fees, staking, governance, DEX trading, and carbon credit transactions. Total supply is fixed at 100 billion tokens.
When will tokens be distributed? ▾
Tokens are distributed at the Token Generation Event (TGE). Presale buyers receive 25% at TGE, with the remaining 75% vested over 12 months after a 3-month cliff. The TGE is scheduled for September 2026.
What is the minimum investment? ▾
The minimum investment for Phase 2 (Presale) is $100 in USDT or ETH. The maximum per wallet is $50,000 to ensure fair distribution. Whitelist members get higher caps.
How do I receive my tokens? ▾
Provide your Verdis Chain wallet address when purchasing. Tokens will be sent to this address at TGE. You can create a wallet at our Verdis Wallet page.
What is the 20% bonus? ▾
During Phase 2 presale, every purchase includes a 20% bonus in VRDX tokens. For example, if you buy 40,000 VRDX for $1,000, you receive 48,000 VRDX total. This bonus is in addition to the already discounted presale price of $0.025 (vs $0.10 listing price).
Is there a lock-up period? ▾
Yes. Presale tokens have a 3-month cliff (no tokens can be transferred) followed by monthly unlocks. 25% unlocks at TGE, then 6.25% per month for 12 months. This prevents immediate dumping and protects token price stability.
