FAQ

How are commissions paid? ▾
Commissions are paid in VRDX tokens directly to your wallet at the Token Generation Event (TGE). You'll receive a single payment covering all tiers with your regular token allocation.
Is there a limit on referrals? ▾
No. You can refer as many people as you want. The more referrals, the more you earn. Top affiliates have earned over $500,000 in VRDX tokens.
Do referrals expire? ▾
Referrals are valid for 90 days after someone clicks your link. If they purchase within that window, you earn commission. After 90 days, the cookie expires.
Can I track my referrals? ▾
Yes. Your dashboard shows total referrals, conversion rate, earnings breakdown by tier, and weekly activity charts. All in real-time.
