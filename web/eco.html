🌱 On-Chain Carbon Credit System

Verdis Chain implements native cVRDX carbon credit tokens. Every cVRDX represents exactly 1 metric ton of verified CO2 greenhouse gas reduction or permanent removal.

🌿

1. Proof-of-Offset Minting

Authorized satellite and IoT telemetry sensors verify real-world offset projects (reforestation, DAC, renewable energy) and mint immutable cVRDX credits directly on-chain.

Pallet-Eco :: mint_credit()
🔄

2. Native AMM Trading

Trade cVRDX credits seamlessly on the native Verdis DEX against VRDX, USDT, or ETH with transparent pricing, instant liquidity, and zero intermediary markups.

Pallet-DEX :: cVRDX/VRDX
🔥

3. Permanent Retirement

Retire cVRDX tokens by burning them on-chain. Burning mints a verifiable cryptographic certificate NFT documenting your net-zero carbon offset achievement.
