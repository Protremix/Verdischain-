How DPoS Works

⚡

BABE Block Production

Validators take turns producing blocks using the BABE (Blind Assignment for Blockchain Extension) VRF lottery, ensuring fair and randomized block production.

🔒

GRANDPA Finality

GHOST-based Recursive ANcestor-Derived Prefixing Agreement provides deterministic finality within 2-3 seconds, preventing chain reorganizations beyond the finalized point.

🌱

Green Validator Score

Validators are scored on renewable energy usage, carbon offset verification, and node efficiency. Higher green scores earn bonus rewards and priority selection.

🛡️

Slashing Protection

Double-signing, equivocation, and downtime trigger automated slashing. Stakers are protected by proportional penalties — never losing more than a defined threshold.

💰

Delegation Rewards

Token holders delegate VRDX to validators and earn 12.4% APY on average. Rewards are distributed automatically each epoch — no claim transaction needed.

🔄

Epoch Rotation

Validator sets rotate every 4 hours (1 epoch = 1,200 blocks). New validators can join the active set based on accumulated stake and green score rankings.
