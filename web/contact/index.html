Feedback Form

Please understand that we can only assist with issues directly related to Verdis Chain services (Verdiscan, DEX, Wallet, Validators). Before writing in, please be informed that:

Pending Transactions

We do not process transactions and are therefore unable to expedite, cancel or replace them. Transactions are finalized by the DPoS validator network.

Verdiscan Block Explorer

Verdiscan is an independent block explorer for the Verdis Chain. We are unable to provide support for issues specific to other blockchains, wallets, or exchanges.

Community Support

We will never ask for your private keys or seed phrases. NEVER send us these. While we try our best to respond in a timely manner, we often have a backlog of inquiries. Please do not spam us.

Wallet / Exchange / Project Issues

Kindly reach out to your wallet service provider, exchange, or project owner for further support, as they are in a better position to assist you on issues related to their platforms.

Send Us a Message

We typically respond within 24–48 hours. For urgent security matters, use the "Security Disclosure" subject.
